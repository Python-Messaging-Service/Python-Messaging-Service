**Python Messaging Service Privacy Policy**  
Effective Date: May 1, 2025

This Privacy Policy outlines how Python Messaging Service ("PMS", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, retains, and protects user data.

**1. Data We Collect**  
We collect only the minimum data necessary to operate the service:
- Username
- Email address
- Metadata about messages (who was messaged and when)
- Account verification status

**2. Message Content**  
Messages are encrypted using **End-to-End ECDH asymmetric encryption**. The content of your messages is **never visible** to our team or stored in a readable format unless:
- A message is reported by a user  
- The reporter provides the decryption key at the time of the report  

**3. Data Retention**  
- If a user deactivates their account, data is retained for **28 days** for security and abuse prevention, after which it is permanently deleted.
- Suspended accounts are also deleted after **28 days**.

**4. Hosting & Third-Party Sharing**  
Our service is hosted on **CodePad.app**, and they may have access to **unencrypted metadata only**. We do **not** share your information with any third parties for advertising or analytics.

**5. Cookies & Tracking**  
PMS does not use persistent cookies or trackers. All client data is cleared immediately upon disconnection.

**6. Children's Privacy**  
PMS is intended for users **aged 13 and over**. We do not collect sensitive data from children. However, we encourage parents to promote safe online habits. No data collected is sufficient to identify or harm a child.

**7. User Rights**  
Users may at any time:
- Request a copy of all data linked to their account  
- Request correction of inaccurate data  
- Request deletion of their account and data (processed within 28 days, which also terminates TOS agreement)

**8. Breach Notification**  
In the event of a security breach:
- All affected users will be notified via email and in-app messaging  
- The breach source will be investigated and patched immediately

**9. Jurisdiction**  
This Privacy Policy is governed under the laws of **England, United Kingdom**.

**10. Commercial Use**  
This policy applies to individual users only. For commercial data use or integration, **please contact pythonmessagingservice@gmail.com** for separate terms.

**11. Changes to This Policy**  
Any updates to this Privacy Policy will be communicated via:
- On-platform message  
- Email notification to affected users

By using Python Messaging Service, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to this Privacy Policy.
